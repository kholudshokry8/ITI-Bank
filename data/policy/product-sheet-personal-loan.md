# Delta Commercial Bank — Product Sheet
## Personal Loan (Salaried Customers) — PL-100

**Document ID:** PS-PL-100 · **Version:** 3.1 · **Effective:** 1 January 2024

### PS-1. Product description
An unsecured instalment loan for salaried employees, repaid in equal monthly
instalments on a reducing-balance basis.

### PS-2. Eligibility
| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Employment type | Salaried, confirmed (not on probation) |
| Minimum net monthly income | EGP 8,000 |
| Minimum age at application | 21 years |
| Salary transfer to Delta | Not required (non-transfer pricing applies) |

### PS-3. Amount and tenor
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum loan amount | EGP 20,000 |
| Maximum loan amount | EGP 1,000,000 |
| Minimum tenor | 12 months |
| Maximum tenor | 60 months, or the maximum permitted by the circular in force, whichever is lower |

### PS-4. Pricing
Interest is charged on a reducing balance. The applicable annual rate is taken
from the pricing table (document PT-2025-01) by tenor band. Rates are fixed for
the life of the loan.

### PS-5. Repayment
- Equal monthly instalments, first instalment due one month after disbursement.
- Instalment formula: `instalment = P x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n)`, where `P` is the
  loan amount, `r` is the annual rate divided by 12, and `n` is the tenor in months.
- Instalments are rounded to two decimal places.

### PS-6. Fees
| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Administrative fee | 1% of the loan amount, deducted at disbursement |
| Early settlement fee | 2% of the outstanding balance |
| Late payment fee | EGP 150 per missed instalment |

### PS-7. Required documents
Application form, salary certificate issued within the last 60 days, national ID
copy, and a credit bureau report obtained by the bank.

### PS-8. Exclusions
This product is not available for: business or commercial purposes, purchase of
securities, or any purpose prohibited by the circulars in force.
