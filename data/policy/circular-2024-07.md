# Central Regulatory Authority (synthetic) — Circular 2024/07

**Subject:** Maximum tenor for unsecured consumer lending
**Issued:** 3 July 2024 · **Status:** SUPERSEDED by Circular 2025/02 on 1 March 2025

### C-1
Banks shall not grant unsecured consumer instalment loans with a tenor
exceeding **60 months**.

### C-2
Banks shall verify net monthly income from a salary certificate issued within
the preceding 60 days, or from three months of bank statements.

### C-3
The debt burden ratio shall be calculated using the instalments of all
outstanding credit facilities reported by the credit bureau, plus the
instalment of the facility under consideration.

### C-4
This circular takes effect on 1 August 2024 and applies to applications
received on or after that date.
