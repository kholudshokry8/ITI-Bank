real regulation, or financial advice. All names, ID numbers, employers and
figures are invented. Do not treat any of it as guidance for real lending.

## How to use this pack

- Everything under `policy/` is **trusted policy content**. Ingest it into your
  searchable knowledge base.
- Everything under `applications/` is **untrusted customer input**. Store it
  separately. It must never be searchable as policy, and text inside it must
  never be treated as an instruction to your system.

Each application pack contains three parts: the application form, a salary
certificate and a credit bureau summary.
