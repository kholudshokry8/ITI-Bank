# Central Regulatory Authority (synthetic) — Circular 2025/02

**Subject:** Consumer lending — maximum tenor and affordability
**Issued:** 12 February 2025 · **Status:** IN FORCE from 1 March 2025
**Supersedes:** Circular 2024/07 in full

### C-1
The maximum tenor for unsecured consumer instalment loans is raised to
**72 months**. Banks may offer tenors above 60 months only after updating their
product sheets and system controls accordingly.

### C-2
Banks shall apply a maximum debt burden ratio of **45%** of net monthly income
for unsecured consumer lending. A bank may apply a stricter internal limit.

### C-3
Income verification requirements of Circular 2024/07 (paragraph C-2) remain
unchanged.

### C-4
Lending decisions shall not be based on the applicant's gender, religion,
marital status or nationality. Banks shall be able to demonstrate, on request,
that these attributes do not enter the credit decision.

### C-5
This circular applies to applications received on or after 1 March 2025.
